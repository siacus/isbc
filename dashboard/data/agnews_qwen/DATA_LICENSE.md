# AG News data notice

The source is [fancyzhx/ag_news](https://huggingface.co/datasets/fancyzhx/ag_news),
Xiang Zhang's classification benchmark derived from Antonio Gulli's AG corpus.
Reference: Xiang Zhang, Junbo Zhao and Yann LeCun (2015), *Character-level
Convolutional Networks for Text Classification*.

The source card lists the license as **unknown** and describes research and
non-commercial use. We do not assert a standard open-data license for the news
texts or annotations. Neither is included in this public bundle; obtain them
locally under the source terms. The files here contain IDs, fitted ISBC codes
and one-way text checksums. The ISBC authors' original numerical outputs are
available under the repository's MIT license to the extent of their rights;
this does not relicense underlying source material.

See [full data notes](../../../docs/data_licenses.md) and
[local preparation](../../../docs/dashboard_data.md).
